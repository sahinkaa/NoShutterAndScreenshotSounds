Silence camera shutter and screenshot sounds regardless of the ringer switch.

### Features

- Mutes photo shutter and screenshot sounds in either ringer-switch position.
- Covers the opening and closing sounds of Night mode exposures.
- One Enabled switch with changes effective immediately without a respring.
- Custom icon in the Settings list and on the settings page.
- Separate standard rootless and native RootHide packages.

### Compatibility

Targets **iOS 15-26**. Tested on **iOS 16 with a standard rootless jailbreak** and **iPhone 11 Pro Max running iOS 26.0.1 with Dopamine 3 RootHide**. iOS 15 and other versions in the range have not been individually device-tested.

Works in Apple's Camera and SpringBoard. Third-party camera apps and AirPods capture cues are outside the confirmed scope. The tweak preserves recorded audio and native video start/stop cues.

### Downloads

- **Standard rootless:** `com.sahinkaa.noshutterscreenshotsounds_1.0_iphoneos-arm64.deb`
- **Native RootHide:** `com.sahinkaa.noshutterscreenshotsounds_1.0_iphoneos-arm64e.deb`

Install only the package matching your jailbreak. Install the native RootHide package directly without RootHide Patcher. Fully close Camera and Settings then respring once after installation. Later changes to Enabled apply immediately.

Source code and SHA-256 checksums are included. To remove the tweak, uninstall it through Sileo then respring and reopen Camera.
